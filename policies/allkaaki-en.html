Alkkagi: Finger Battle Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026

Alkkagi: Finger Battle (hereinafter "the App") respects your privacy. The App does not require account registration, login credentials, payment details, or personal location information. All game state and user preferences are processed locally and securely on your device.

1. Information Collected

The App does not collect or transmit any of the following personal data to external servers:

  • Personal identifiers such as name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • Location information (GPS location or location history)
  • Payment data, account credentials, or contacts/address book
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID is explicitly removed and prohibited in the app)

However, to diagnose unexpected crashes and optimize user experience, the App may use product analytics and crash reporting tools (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, PostHog) strictly in release builds to collect anonymized usage events, crash logs, device models, and OS version information that cannot identify any individual.

2. Nearby Connections (Wireless P2P Match) & Permissions

The App provides a "Nearby Wireless Battle" feature allowing two players in the same physical space to play in real-time. The following device features and permissions are utilized strictly for this functionality:

  • Nearby Devices & Bluetooth Permissions (BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_ADVERTISE, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES): Used solely to discover nearby opponent devices and establish a direct peer-to-peer connection. These permissions are explicitly marked with neverForLocation and are never used to track your physical location. All game moves and state data exchanged between devices are discarded immediately upon disconnecting.
  • Vibration Permission (VIBRATE): Used exclusively to provide tactile haptic feedback during stone collisions and match outcomes locally on your device.

3. Device-Side Processing (Local Storage)

Game settings, custom board selections, stone placement layouts, and sound/haptic toggles are stored strictly on your local device storage. This data is never sent to any external server and can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing app data.

4. Third-Party Services

The App utilizes the following third-party services for local wireless multiplayer connectivity and anonymous analytics. Each service operates under its respective privacy policy:

  • Google Play Services (Nearby Connections) Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy Policy
  • PostHog Privacy Policy

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). The App does not require account creation and is designed to provide a safe, family-friendly gaming experience for all ages.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

The App does not maintain any centralized user databases or server logs. Local game preferences remain on your device and are permanently deleted when you uninstall the application.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable legal requirements. Any updates will be published directly on this page.

NoticeFlow Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

NoticeFlow (Korean: 알림결, hereinafter "the App") is an Android notification management utility that dismisses or snoozes notifications based on user-defined rules. The App does not require user accounts, payment details, contacts, or location access.

Core Privacy Principle: Notification titles and body text are inspected strictly in memory on your device to evaluate matching rules. Notification content is never saved to history, logged, or transmitted to external servers.

1. Information Not Collected

  • Personal identifiers such as name, email address, or phone number
  • Notification titles, body text, sender content, or rule keyword content in history, logs, or analytics
  • Precise or coarse location data
  • Contacts, call logs, or SMS messages
  • Advertising identifiers (both AD_ID and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID are explicitly removed from the merged manifest)

2. Permissions & On-Device Storage

  • Notification Access (BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE): Active only after you explicitly grant Notification Access in Android System Settings. Used to evaluate posted notifications in memory and call cancelNotification or snoozeNotification for matching rules.
  • Post Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, Android 13+ optional): Requested only if you use the local test notification diagnostic button in Settings. NoticeFlow ignores its own notifications to prevent loops.
  • Launcher App Query: Queries installed launcher apps locally on-device to populate the rule editor's app dropdown. The list of installed apps is never uploaded or included in analytics.
  • Local Rules & Metadata History: Your rules and up to the latest 100 execution metadata records (rule display name, target app/package, timestamp, action, and success/failure) are stored locally in app-private DataStore Preferences. History contains no notification text or keywords and can be cleared anytime in the History screen or by clearing app data.

3. Diagnostics & Analytics (Release Builds Only)

Release builds collect minimal pseudonymous operational metadata for stability and app lifecycle visibility. Debug builds disable Firebase and PostHog collection completely. Session replay and automatic screen capture are disabled.

  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Crash reports, device model, OS version, app version, and app-open lifecycle events (app_opened).
  • PostHog (US Cloud): App-open lifecycle events (app_opened) and Settings "More Apps" banner impressions/clicks. No notification content, target packages, or rule keywords are included.

Third-party privacy policies:

4. Remote Config & External Links

The Settings screen may display cross-promotion recommendations fetched via Firebase Remote Config. Tapping a card opens Google Play or an official developer showcase link.

5. Children's Privacy

NoticeFlow is a general-audience utility app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

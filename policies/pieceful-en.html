Pieceful Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

Pieceful (hereinafter "the App") is a mindful jigsaw puzzle application designed to provide relaxing, contemplative puzzling. The App does not require account registration, payment information, contact access, or location tracking.

Key Privacy Guarantee: Images selected for custom puzzles are processed strictly on your local device in app-private storage. Personal photos, filenames, and puzzle layouts are never uploaded to any remote server.

1. Information Not Collected

Pieceful values user privacy and does not collect or store the following:

  • Personal identifiers such as name, email address, or phone number
  • Precise or coarse location information
  • Financial or payment account details
  • Contact lists, call logs, or address book records
  • Advertising identifiers (AD_ID permission is removed)

2. Photos and On-Device Data Storage

  • Photo Picker: The App accesses only the images you explicitly select via the system Photo Picker to create custom puzzles.
  • App-Private Storage: Selected images are resized and stored locally in app-private storage. Original photo metadata (including GPS coordinates) is stripped.
  • Zero Server Upload: Personal photos, puzzle pieces, and game progress are never sent to any server or included in analytics payloads.
  • Deletion: Deleting a custom puzzle removes its local cached copy. Your original photo in your device gallery remains completely untouched.

3. Diagnostics and Analytics (Third-Party Services)

In release builds, minimal pseudonymous diagnostics are used to ensure stability and improve app quality:

  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Crash logs, device model, OS version, app version, and pseudonymous app installation IDs.
  • PostHog: High-level gameplay statistics such as piece count (e.g., 20 or 1,000 pieces), source type (curated vs. custom photo), and elapsed puzzle time. No personal data or images are sent.

For more details on third-party privacy practices:

  • Google Firebase Privacy
  • PostHog Privacy Policy

4. Remote Configuration and Offline Play

Firebase Remote Config may be used for guidance messages and recommended apps. Tapping a recommendation opens the Google Play Store. Puzzling and custom photo features function completely offline.

5. Children's Privacy

Pieceful is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

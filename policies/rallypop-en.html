RallyPop Privacy Policy

Effective date 2026-11-21 · RallyPop

Scope and contact

RallyPop is a sports scorekeeping app provided by Seokwon Jeong (Sangcomz). This policy applies to the Android phone and tablet app and its compatible Wear OS app. Contact hello.seokwon@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Information stored on your devices

The app stores player names or nicknames, roster entries, sport and match rules, points, set results, match times, the current match, and display, sound and timer preferences in app-specific storage. It uses these records to show match history and head-to-head results and restore ongoing matches. You can use nicknames for players.

Developer servers and analytics

The current app does not send match content or player names to a developer-operated server. It has no registration, app accounts, advertising, in-app purchases, advertising identifiers, usage analytics SDK or crash analytics SDK. Firebase App Distribution distributes test app files and is not bundled as an SDK collecting player information.

Phone and watch synchronization

When the same app is used on compatible paired Android and Wear OS devices, Google Play services Wearable Data Layer exchanges current match state, player names, scores, match settings, scoring commands, synchronization identifiers and timestamps. Transfers may use Bluetooth or route through Google servers depending on connectivity. Google documents encrypted Bluetooth transport and end-to-end encryption for traffic routed through Google Cloud. The developer does not receive the decryption keys or transferred content. Match information is exchanged to display and control the match on your connected devices.

Android backup and device transfer

The app allows Android backup. Depending on your system backup and device-transfer settings and your device provider, app data may be backed up or restored to another device. These operations follow the Android and backup provider settings and policies. The developer does not receive your backup files or operate a backup restoration service.

Permissions and external pages

The phone app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or photo access. The Wear OS app may use system features to keep the screen awake and provide haptic feedback. Opening a privacy or other external link uses your selected browser and the destination website policies. This policy page contains no advertising or visitor analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process connection information to serve web requests.

Retention and deletion

You can delete match records and roster entries through the app. Clearing RallyPop storage in Android settings removes app data on that device. Clear watch data separately on the watch. System backups and restored copies are managed through system backup and transfer settings. App data is retained until you delete it or reset app storage.

Email support

If you contact us by email, your sender address and message are used to respond to and follow up on your request. Send only the information needed for support. Correspondence is retained for as long as needed to handle the request, and deletion requests can be sent to the same email address.

Changes

We update this page and its effective date when app features or data practices change. Material changes are also communicated through the relevant feature or an in-app notice.

Swidy Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026 · Package: xyz.sangcomz.cleaner

Swidy, provided by Sangcomz (정석원), is an Android app for reviewing and organizing screenshots and photos. Its former name was Gallery Diet. No account or login is required.

1. Photo access and processing on your device

With the photo access you grant, Swidy reads the device photo library, file sizes and information used to identify screenshots. It displays photos for review, lets you keep them or add them to a deletion queue, and stores cleanup counts and recovered-space statistics locally. Photo files and photo content are not uploaded to external servers for sorting.

Adding a photo to the queue does not immediately delete it. Final deletion follows the system confirmation process on supported Android versions. You can change or revoke photo access in device settings.

2. Usage analytics

Release builds use PostHog to understand usage and improve the app. Interaction events such as app lifecycle events and screen views, analytics identifiers, app version, and technical information about the device, operating system and language may be transmitted to the analytics service. Because analytics identifiers are used, this information should not be described as fully anonymous.

When the analytics service's default location enrichment is enabled, it may infer approximate country- or city-level location from an IP address. Swidy does not request precise GPS location. The current app does not offer a separate user setting to enable or disable usage analytics.

Photo content, contacts, payment information and user account information are not collected for usage analytics. If you contact us by email, we may process your email address and message to respond.

3. Purposes and external processing service

Usage analytics are used to understand app usage and improve the user experience. PostHog is the current analytics service; data is sent to its US hosting endpoint over HTTPS. Local photo processing and transmission of usage metadata are separate activities.

  • PostHog Privacy Policy

The current verified Swidy configuration does not include Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics or Firebase Remote Config. If this changes, the policy will be updated to reflect the actual processing.

4. Local storage and security

Cleanup statistics and necessary app state are stored on your device. Photos remain in the device photo library. Clearing Swidy's app data or uninstalling the app is separate from deleting photo files. Analytics transmission uses HTTPS.

5. Retention and deletion

Local app state can be reset using Android's clear-app-data feature. This does not delete gallery photos or automatically erase information already transmitted to the analytics service. Analytics data is retained according to the analytics project's retention settings. Automatic deletion within 90 days is not guaranteed.

For questions about how data is handled, contact the email below.

6. Policy updates

When app functionality or data handling changes, we update this page and its effective date.

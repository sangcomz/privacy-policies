VPN Quest Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Effective date 2026-11-24 · VPN Quest

1. Scope and contact

VPN Quest is a WireGuard VPN client utility for Android provided by Seokwon Jeong (Sangcomz). This policy and terms apply to the Android app (xyz.sangcomz.vpnquest). For privacy or support questions, contact hello.seokwon@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

For your WireGuard connection management and convenience, the app stores the following data only in app-private storage on your device:

  • WireGuard profiles entered or imported via .conf files or QR codes (private key, peer public key, endpoint, allowed IPs, DNS, and split-tunneling excluded app list)
  • All saved profiles are encrypted with an Android Keystore AES-GCM key.

Encrypted profile storage is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer via allowBackup="false" and data_extraction_rules.xml. Screen capture is disabled via FLAG_SECURE. Non-identifying connection lifecycle logs (timestamps, phases, exit codes) are held only in an in-memory ring buffer (up to 200 lines) that you can copy or clear.

3. WireGuard VPN communication

The app uses Android VpnService and a Rust/BoringTun engine to establish an encrypted tunnel directly with the WireGuard server you configure. The developer does not operate VPN servers or provide VPN accounts, and never uploads your private keys, server addresses, IP addresses, or network traffic to developer servers. Unconfigured IPv6 traffic may be blocked to prevent leaks, and server-side logging depends solely on the operator of the WireGuard server you connect to.

4. Usage analytics, diagnostics, and app discovery

Release builds use PostHog through its US cloud endpoint (https://us.i.posthog.com), Google Analytics for Firebase, and Firebase Crashlytics to improve app stability and understand feature reliability. Telemetry is limited to non-identifying events such as app launches, connection attempt/success/failure codes, profile save/delete actions, promotion card interactions, and crash diagnostics.

WireGuard server addresses, endpoints, profile names, private keys, IP addresses, DNS settings, and network traffic are never included in analytics events or crash reports. PostHog automatic screen/deep-link capture and session replay are disabled. Android Advertising ID (AD_ID), ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION permissions are removed, and Firebase advertising ID collection, ad-personalization signals, and ad storage are disabled. All analytics and Crashlytics collection are disabled in development/debug builds.

The home screen may display a promotion card linking to other developer apps or web pages; tapping it opens Google Play or your browser. There is no account creation, in-app purchase, or paid feature.

5. Permissions and external links

The app requests only network access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE), foreground tunnel execution (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE), connection status notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS), and VPN interface binding (BIND_VPN_SERVICE). App-level split tunneling queries only launcher apps via a scoped <queries> declaration rather than QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES, and QR import uses Google Play Services Code Scanner without requesting CAMERA or location permissions. External links open in your default browser or Google Play and follow those services’ policies. This policy page contains no advertising or visitor analytics scripts.

6. Retention and deletion

Deleting a profile, resetting the vault, clearing app storage in Android settings, or uninstalling VPN Quest removes all encrypted profiles and keys from your device. Pseudonymous analytics and crash diagnostics already transmitted are retained according to provider retention policies; inquiries may be sent to the contact email.

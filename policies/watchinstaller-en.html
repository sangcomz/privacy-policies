Watch APK Installer Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Effective date 2026-11-24 · Watch APK Installer (WatchInstaller)

1. Scope and contact

Watch APK Installer (WatchInstaller) is an Android utility provided by Seokwon Jeong (Sangcomz) to wirelessly install and manage APKs on Wear OS watches. This policy and terms apply to the Android app (xyz.sangcomz.watchinstaller). For privacy or support questions, contact hello.seokwon@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

For convenience and wireless ADB (TLS) authentication, the app stores only the following data in app-private storage:

  • Last connected watch IP address, connection port, and initial pairing status (DataStore)
  • A locally generated 2048-bit RSA private key and self-signed X.509 certificate (adb_soft_key.pk8, adb_soft_cert.der) used for wireless ADB pairing and connection

The key and certificate files are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer via backup_rules.xml and data_extraction_rules.xml. Selected APK files may be copied briefly to app cache to inspect package metadata (app label, package name, version) or stream to the watch and are deleted immediately afterward.

3. Local Wi-Fi discovery and wireless ADB transfer

The app uses Android NsdManager (mDNS services _adb-tls-pairing._tcp. and _adb-tls-connect._tcp.) to discover your Wear OS watch with Wireless Debugging enabled on the same Wi-Fi network. Pairing, connection, APK streaming, listing user packages, and uninstall commands occur directly between your phone and your watch over your local network. APK files, IP addresses, ports, and pairing codes are never uploaded to developer servers.

4. Usage analytics, diagnostics, and app discovery

Release builds use PostHog through its US cloud endpoint (https://us.i.posthog.com), Google Analytics for Firebase, and Firebase Crashlytics to understand feature reliability and diagnose crashes. Events are limited to non-identifying telemetry such as app opens, step transitions, pairing/connect/install/uninstall outcomes, and opening the guide, privacy policy, or More Apps actions.

Watch IP addresses, ports, 6-digit pairing codes, APK filenames or paths, package names, and private keys are never sent in analytics events or crash reports. PostHog automatic screen/deep-link capture, session replay, person-profile creation, and GeoIP lookup are disabled. Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) and AdServices Advertising ID permissions are removed, and Firebase advertising identifier collection and ad-personalization signals are disabled. All analytics and Crashlytics collection are disabled in development/debug builds.

Selecting More Apps opens the developer’s app list on Google Play. After a successful installation, the app may also request Google Play’s official In-App Review flow subject to cooldown and cap rules. Google Play is governed by Google’s privacy policy. There is no account creation, in-app purchase, or paid feature.

5. Permissions and external links

The app requests only network access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) and local multicast discovery (CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE). It does not request camera, location, contacts, phone state, or broad storage permissions; APK selection relies on Android’s system file picker (SAF). External links open in your browser or Google Play and follow those services’ policies. This policy page contains no advertising or visitor analytics scripts.

6. Retention and deletion

Clearing Watch APK Installer storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app removes all saved watch target settings and local ADB credentials from your device. Pseudonymous analytics and crash diagnostics already transmitted are retained according to provider retention policies; requests may be sent to the contact email.
